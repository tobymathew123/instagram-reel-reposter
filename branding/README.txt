PUT YOUR CANVA FRAME HERE
=========================

Save your Canva design in this folder with the exact name:

    frame.png

Requirements:
  - Size: 1080 x 1920 pixels (Canva "Instagram Reel" / "Your Story" size)
  - File type: PNG with "Transparent background" ticked
  - The middle must be see-through, otherwise it will cover the video

"example-frame.png" is a sample. To try the system before your own design is ready,
make a copy of it and rename the copy to frame.png.

After adding or changing frame.png you do NOT need to restart anything -
the next Reel automatically uses the new file.
